# Save files
*Save files can be representations of PTSD dejavu. They leak and they repeat, and sometimes, when it’s safe, a trigger leaks from another save file, or even from real life into a save file… and in some cases… it hurts you from a save file right into real life.*
## Omnitemporal bugs
You can rewind a save file back to its original at any time… you can also create several copies and play the game in that state how many times you want, that’s why it gets really unsettling when you encounter the first omnitemporal bug. They are bugs that won’t rewind, they’ll stay there, unaware of the rewinds or copies… they’ll have a linear perception of time.
Solving them:
- Some are easy: just keep playing the save file and they’ll eventually be gone, because of their linear perception of time. They are just passing by and exiting around.
- Some of them need something: they won’t go until you give them the item they want.
- Some of them want to see change: they won’t go until you do something in the save file. It could be revenge: hurt a character in the save file. It could be worry: help a character in the save file. It could be greedy: bury a treasure from a diseased character.
- Some of them are moles: they observe you and follow you around the save files. If you make the right choices, they’ll trust you and give you information, or ingame items. This ones don’t require fixing, they are harmless and save file owners are unaware of them.
- Some of them could be scary: idk how, but they could intimidate you into doing stuff in real life. (as in the protagonist’s real life).
- Some of them can be a real character living in a Save File… the difference is that: they remember everything. You can make a Save File corrupt if you are not careful around them, when this happens, you need to report to TRS the save file as lost, and the owner will have to start over again. They won’t be happy about this.
    - Some of them could beg you not to report the corruption. They are scared of death.
