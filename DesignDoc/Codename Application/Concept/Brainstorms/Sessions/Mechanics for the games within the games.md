# How do you reach this games?
it’s funny coz you can reach them inside of the game that you are QAing
but you could also go to the bar and play on the arcade, and inside the arcade there should be a computer with access to another game
or even… interactive objects that trigger minigames: like “Every time I walk my dog it disappears :/”, so you grab his savefile and he actually has a dogo lol, and you interact with it and it takes you to the walking dogo minigame… and inside the walking dogo minigame you could find other games.
If you try to exit the game while walking the dogo, the dogo walk is aborted and you return to the game you are QAing.
This will make it very hard to remember how they got to a certain game lol, they’ll forget and play and replay other save files until they finally remember how to enter a certain game they are looking for.
The gameplay can be non-linear, we can just hide a bunch of stuff everywhere and see how they figure it out and piece it all together. They’ll discover new things and use the information or glitches they unlocked on another things, which will unlock and disclose more glitches and information.
The game could have an ending, you could easily finish it from A to Z, but before reaching the ending you could go around and trigger a lot of funny stuff and weird information about the world, the protagonist and the game studio.
I am not scared about people not finding something that I’ve hidden in there… i want to provide a seamless experience, and i know that in the making of one, a lot of things can go wrong and a lot of my work can go unnoticed… I’ll inlcude it in my portfolio, apply for jobs with it and call it a day :3
