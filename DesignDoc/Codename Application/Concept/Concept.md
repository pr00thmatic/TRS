# A quick intro to the story
You flip burgers, that’s your job. You are eventually fired or your employer goes bankrupt, whatever happens first. You get home, sad, tired, unemployed and uncertain about the future. A note reaches you from beneath your door: The Redacted Studio is looking for QAs… weird… you have no background, but you bite, you are already desperate. You start tomorrow, 8:00 am, turn on your computer (the screen now takes the whole space).
This “real” part: you don’t notice it, you just feel like you are playing some kind of weirdly decided resident evil-like camera-follow-up game… it feels weird… and that’s because i’ll make it so the camera is actually “creeping around you”… spying you… it’s fixed to the top corner of your room… walking through the street? it side-follows you (it’s 3D, but the angle is 2D, flat, like A Night in the Woods)… [sometimes interrupted by the trashcans at the other side of the streets (from where the camera is creeping at you from)](https://www.notion.so/Ways-of-breaking-the-3rd-wall-2091ba1f792880b080f9ef88d4276f2f?pvs=21). You go to the kitchen to eat something? the camera abruptly switches to the kitchen, pointing at you from a different corner. The bathroom has no camera: you enter the bathroom, nothing happens, it just enters, flushes after a few seconds, and the protagonist comes out.
So… you turn on your computer, the screen now takes the whole “real-player” screen: there’s Discord, there’s GMail… they send the game exes and the QA tool exe… you receive a save file, put it in the desktop, you can drag it into the game to open it. The game opens, the QA tools are active as an overlay in your screen. You play the game seamlessly this way.
Sometimes you’ll find games inside of the games you are already playing, the transition will be almost the same, except, in the game you just “Press A” when close to a thing to interact with it, some of the things you interact with will transition you into the game unceremoneusly.
## The Surveiller
the protagonist... the camera.... this "reality"... this is just another game inside another game... a meta game you don't know you are inside of...
there will come a time in which, when you go Menu -> Exit at this "real" game... the game won't exit... the real player is actually expecting to stop playing... he won't.... he exit the game into this outter meta-game... you are spying the protagonist and now are an employee inside of The Redacted Studio, looking at the surveillance cameras
so... when they exit... the "Exit" overlay is hovering, transparent background, you can still see the protagonist...
so... the screen will acquire a CRT filter and the camera will pan-out revealing a screen... and will keep zooming out... until a dark room can be seen, and a whole lot of other CRT screens are around that CRT screen
i am still undecided... i should create a brainstorm for this... I don't want to give too much mechanical depth to this bit... i need to complete the story and give this moment the importance it deserves...
it'll just be a narrative resource... a piece of hidden story meaning and development... maybe a little bit of play-time... with a small decision that can't be re-made... and after you take that decision you are left with "oh shit... what did i just did? why did i do that? i didn't meant to... it just... seemed like what the game was asking from me... let me go back... oh shit, i can't... i can't change my decision"
