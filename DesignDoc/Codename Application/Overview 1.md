# Goal
## Specific
Create a thriller game in which you are a QA featuring games inside games inside games that constantly break the 3rd wall and provide an unbreaking smooth experience to the player no matter the decisions they make.
## Measurable and Attainable
Must be:
- Published on Steam
- 2-3h of gameplay
- 500 ~ 1k downloads
- 60%+ Positive reviews

## Relevant
Laboral criticism is relevant
## Time-bound
Made in 3 years if employed, 1 year if unemployed.
